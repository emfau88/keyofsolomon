The 1889 Redway edition

A scarce first printing, made accessible again.

The title page identifies S. Liddell MacGregor Mathers as translator and editor and George Redway of London as publisher. The surviving scan also preserves the original notice that only 500 copies were produced.

This edition does not pretend to be one of those originals. It is a restored modern production built directly from that historical printing and produced one copy at a time, on demand.

Source basis: digitized 1889 copy from the Wellcome / Internet Archive collection.
