Is this a modern rewrite?

No. The product is based on the 1889 S. Liddell MacGregor Mathers edition. The objective is restoration of that printing, not rewriting its language or replacing its historical plates.

Because the product is the physical edition: hardcover format, restored reproduction, original plates and collector presentation. The underlying historical text remains public domain.

Yes. The product direction is fixed as a near-black hardcover with antique-gold typography and a warm cream interior. Red cover variants are no longer part of the product.
